Frequently asked

What is OASIS?

OASIS is a private collection of luxury villas in Bali, rented by the night: architect-designed villas across the island, each with a dedicated concierge. We rent villas; we don't sell them.

How do I book a stay?

Press Book on any villa (or Book Your Stay above), choose your dates and guests, then Continue to WhatsApp. Your request opens in WhatsApp ready to send, and our team replies to confirm availability and the final rate.

How much does a night cost?

Each villa card shows its nightly rate. The booking form shows an estimated total for your dates.

Do I pay on the website?

No. Nothing is charged online. Once your stay is confirmed on WhatsApp, our team arranges payment with you directly.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse or book a stay. A free member account adds full villa details, meetings with a villa specialist, private villa viewings, e-tickets and reviews. Create an account

What are meetings and viewings?

A meeting is a private consultation with a villa specialist, in Seminyak or online, to plan your stay. A viewing is a private visit to a villa with a chauffeur. Members book both here and receive an e-ticket.

Which time zone do you use?

Bali time (WITA, UTC+8) everywhere on the site, whatever time zone you are in.

How can I reach you?

WhatsApp or call +62 821-7980-8686, or email oasis@gmail.com.
